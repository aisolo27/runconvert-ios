# RunConvert Support

Draft for a public page. Add the real public support email or contact form before publishing.

RunConvert converts running pace, distance, finish times, target paces, and workouts. Everything runs on your phone, without an account or internet connection.

For pace, enter a value like 5:00. You can also type 500 and the app formats it for you. Distance fields support decimals. Tap KM or MI to change units. In Workout, choose a normal run or intervals and tap Convert to see the summary.
