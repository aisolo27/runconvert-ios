Privacy policy

Updated September 27, 2026

RunConvert works offline. Your pace, distance, time, and workout values are processed on your device. The app does not send those values to me or anyone else.

Data in the app

RunConvert does not collect personal data, track you, use analytics or advertising SDKs, or require an account. It does not access Apple Health, location, contacts, photos, or your microphone.

Inputs are held in memory while the app is running. The app does not save a workout history or upload your entries. Clear resets the current converter. There is no server-side account or stored workout data to delete.

Apple services

Apple may separately process download, App Store, or diagnostic information under its own settings and privacy policy.

If you contact me

If you email support, I receive your email address and the information you choose to send so I can respond. Your message is handled by the email services involved and may remain in the support mailbox. You can email me to request deletion of a support conversation. Please don't send sensitive information or personal workout details you don't want to share.

This website

This site is hosted on Vercel. Vercel may process technical request information, including IP addresses, browser information, and request logs, to deliver and secure the site. This website does not add analytics, advertising scripts, or tracking cookies. Website requests are separate from the offline app's conversions.

See Vercel's privacy notice for its practices.

Changes and contact

If the app's privacy practices change, I'll update this policy and its date. For privacy questions or support, contact aisolorzano98@hotmail.com.
